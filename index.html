◈ I ◈

Four Stones

Four stones sit in the lintel. One was carved; three are mute. Give the mute their voices, as they were when the house was young. The carved one speaks a tongue of emperors, and it was never what it seems. Do not paint the walls in blood, nor in the sun, but in the breath between the two.

XVIII
✦ ✦ ✦
One flame. One chance.

The stones forgive three wrong turns. The flame forgives none: you have one chance at the color. A wrong color shifts the sands, rearranges every color, and jams the lock at once. Every wrong guess makes the wait longer, up to 5 minutes (10 for the flame), and leaving the page will not wash it away.

Imagine thinking it would be that easy.

Shame.Shame.Shame.
